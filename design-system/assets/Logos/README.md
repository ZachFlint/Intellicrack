The Intellicrack brand marks: a blue brain (`intellicrack-mark`, shipped as splash-icon.png) on a near-black ground, and the white monospace `intellicrack-wordmark`. The mark's blue is about #07a2fe; the ground runs #0d0d0d to #151515. `app-icon` is the 256px frame of the Windows icon.ico (which also carries 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64 and 128px frames). `splash` is the 1024px still artwork that build_splash_image() composites: a radial #1a1a1a to #0a0a0a vignette, the brain at y=288 and the frozen wordmark centred at y=624. Use them as shipped; where a mark cannot be placed, set the name in the code font (white on the dark ground) rather than redrawing the brain.
