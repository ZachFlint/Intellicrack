Text entry. Line edits, combos and spin boxes share a 4px radius and switch their border to `ic-accent` on focus; a disabled field drops to the `ic-header` fill with muted text. Line edits sit on the `ic-field` colour, combos and spin boxes on `ic-combo`, and multi-line text edits render on the `ic-content` ground in the monospace face. Text selection is the one `ic-selection` blue.
