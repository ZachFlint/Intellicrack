Dock title bars, splitter handles and line separators. A dock title is a 6px-padded `ic-header` strip with a hairline `ic-border` underneath and left-aligned `ic-text`; the stylesheet blanks the title-bar float and close icons. Splitter handles are 8px (`ic-splitter`) bars in `ic-border` that turn `ic-accent` under the pointer (the vertical handle on the right). Horizontal and vertical line frames draw in `ic-border`.
