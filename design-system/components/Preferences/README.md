The Preferences dialog. A `preferences_category_list` on the recessed `ic-panel-muted` strip, split from the page by a hairline `ic-border`; each category is padded 12px 16px, hovers to `ic-item-hover` (Tools) and selects to `ic-selection` (Appearance). The `preferences_stack` page sits on the window ground and holds ordinary group boxes and controls. The `preferences_button_bar` runs across the bottom on `ic-chrome` with a top hairline.
