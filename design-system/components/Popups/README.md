Open and compound controls. An open combo drops an `ic-chrome` list framed in `ic-border` whose current item fills with `ic-selection`. Spin-box arrows sit in 16px `ic-control-raised` buttons that lighten to `ic-control-raised-hover`. The `tool_menu_button` (a panel toolbar's drop-down QToolButton) is transparent with an `ic-border` outline and a right-hand menu arrow; it hovers to `ic-control-raised` with an `ic-accent` edge, presses to `ic-tool-pressed` and disables to `ic-text-disabled`.
