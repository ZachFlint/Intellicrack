The animated startup splash (SplashScreen, 600x400 logical pixels), rebuilt from the constants in splash_screen.py at 55% progress. A three-stop linear gradient rotates at 12 degrees per second while each stop cycles through three dark HSV colours (navy, violet, teal at 16 to 18% value) on a 21-second sine. The brain mark is 30% of the height, centred at 24%; "INTELLICRACK" sits at 50% in the 32pt bold heading font, ringed by a glow of 32 `#007acc` copies (four radii, eight directions, alpha 80 down to 15). An eight-stage pipeline (Creds, Providers, Tools, Session, Engine, Scripts, Models, UI) runs 50px from the bottom: completed stages are filled accent circles with a white tick, the active stage pulses its ring and centre dot, pending stages are #555555 outlines and a failed stage turns #cc3333 with a white cross. The status message is painted above the pipeline and the version at the bottom right. Once the first progress update arrives, a 6px progress bar (#007acc chunk on a #3e3e42 track, 3px radius, 20px side margins) appears in the gap between the status text and the pipeline row, 6px above the top of the circles; it is the only overlay element, so nothing is drawn twice. All splash metrics are logical pixels, so the layout holds at any display scale. The splash uses fixed colours, not theme tokens: #007acc accent, #d4d4d4 text, #3e3e42 track.
