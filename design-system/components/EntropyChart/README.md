The hex editor's statistics charts (EntropyGraphWidget and ByteDistributionWidget), drawn from the real per-block entropy and byte counts of notepad.exe. The entropy chart lays three bands over `ic-chart-bg` (`ic-chart-band-low` 0 to 3.5, `-mid` to 6.5, `-high` to 8) and draws each segment of the line in `ic-entropy-low`, `-mid` or `-high` by its starting value, inside a 1px `ic-chart-axis` frame with 4px padding. The byte histogram draws 256 bars in `ic-chart-bar`, the bar under the pointer in `ic-chart-bar-hover`, with a toggle between linear and logarithmic scale; on a typical PE the zero-byte bar dwarfs the rest at linear scale. Section entropies are listed underneath for reference.
