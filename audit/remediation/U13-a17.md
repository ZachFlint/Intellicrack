TinyLlama-1.1B-Chat model on the Intel Arc B580 XPU (no mocks, no synthetic
responses). New module-level helpers provide independent oracles:

  control-character noise fails every sub-gate.

- `_max_char_run_ratio` - repetition-collapse detector.
- `_seed_all` - seeds CPU/XPU/CUDA torch generators for determinism.
