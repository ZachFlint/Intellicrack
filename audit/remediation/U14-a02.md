actual signal connection, not just the slot body. Fixtures are typed
`Config`/`Orchestrator`; the embedded-tool tab page is taken from the public
`embedded_tools` dict (a real `QWidget`) for `indexOf`/close, and the
disconnect test uses the public `address_clicked.disconnect()`.
