## Notes on approach

- No `unittest.mock` / `MagicMock` / `patch` of any operation under test. The
  only substitutions are the OS-modal `QMessageBox` static methods (recorded,
  not shown) and the `SandboxManager`/async session dispatch, neither of which
  is the behavior being verified.
- A real minimal 64-bit PE (`MZ` + `e_lfanew` + `PE\0\0` + AMD64
  `IMAGE_FILE_HEADER` + `IMAGE_OPTIONAL_HEADER64` magic) is built on disk and
  driven through the real routing and the real hexcore-backed editor.
- Determinism: tail-reader writes are fsync-ed and waited on by condition;
  async-bridge timing uses a measured elapsed-time oracle and `threading.Event`
  sentinels; QSettings isolation removes order-dependent tab restoration. The
  full four-file suite was run twice and passed 39/39 both times.
