## Notes

- A real production defect was surfaced while building these gates: rizin's
  `/xj` emits per-hit keys under `address` (e.g. `{"address":16,...}`), but
  `search_bytes` (and the sibling `search_bytes_wildcard` / ROP paths) parse
  `_get_int(r, "offset")`, so the returned per-match **addresses collapse to 0**
  while the match **count** is correct. The High search gates therefore assert
  the exact, independently-known match **counts** and str/bytes equivalence
  (all correct in production and fully falsifiable), and intentionally do not
  freeze the buggy address values. Fixing the address-key parse is a `src`
  change outside this test-only unit and is recorded here for follow-up; the
  rewritten tests remain genuine gates over the encoding, space-stripping, and
  end-to-end search-count behaviour they claim to cover.
